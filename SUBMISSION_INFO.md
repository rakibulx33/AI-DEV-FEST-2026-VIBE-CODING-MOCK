**Project title:** Smart Escape — Interactive Evacuation Route Simulator

**Description / abstract:** Smart Escape is a browser-based evacuation route simulator. Import a building JSON file, choose a starting room or junction, and see the lowest-cost route to an open exit on an interactive map. Block locations or corridors, close exits, and the route updates immediately or explains why no route is available. It supports English and Bangla, validates input, and runs entirely in the browser without external routing services.
